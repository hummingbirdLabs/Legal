Privacy Policy

Application: GpuAi  ·  Effective date: October 10, 2026  ·  Controller: HummingbirdLabs

Short summary: GpuAi is a fully offline GPU toolkit. It inspects your graphics hardware, assesses game and AI readiness, runs local self-tests, and shows an in-game overlay — all of it on your device. The application declares no internet-access capability and performs no network communication of its own. The hardware information it reads, your settings, and your exported reports never leave your device and are never transmitted to us. We do not have access to your device data or any personal data.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, process, store, or transmit any personal data. GpuAi is a fully offline desktop application distributed through the Microsoft Store, and its application package declares no internet-access capability (internetClient is not requested). The only network-related operations are license and entitlement checks performed by Windows itself through the system Store APIs (see Section 5); the application contains no other networking code.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal details such as name, email address, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs

  • Usage statistics, analytics, or telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Browsing history, files, or account credentials

3. Hardware and Device Information Read Locally

To provide its inspection, readiness assessment, self-test, and overlay features, GpuAi reads technical information about your computer exclusively on your local device, including:

  • Graphics adapter identification: GPU model, vendor, VRAM size, and display driver version (driver version is read from local Windows device registry entries)
  • Real-time GPU telemetry from the libraries installed with your graphics driver: values such as utilization, temperature, fan speed, power draw, clock speeds, and memory usage
  • The total amount of physical system memory (RAM) installed, read through a read-only Windows system API
  • The results of a brief local rendering self-test performed by the graphics driver

GPU telemetry is obtained through the vendor-provided runtime components that ship with your graphics driver (NVIDIA NVML, AMD ADLX, and Intel IGCL). These components are installed by the driver, not by GpuAi; GpuAi calls them locally on your machine to display readings. None of the information above is logged to disk by default, assembled into a profile, sent over the network, or shared with any third party. It is processed in memory for the sole purpose of showing results inside the application and in the optional in-game overlay.

4. Local Data Storage

All data the application writes stays in a single local folder on your device: %LocalAppData%\GpuAi\. It contains only:

  • settings.json – small application preferences you choose, such as theme and language
  • license.json – a local entitlement document used only by the development/simulation back end in unpackaged development builds; in the installed Store version, license state is managed by Windows itself

Exported reports: When you choose to export an inspection report, a PNG image is written only to the location you select in the save dialog. GpuAi does not keep, upload, or back up your reports.

The in-game overlay: The optional overlay simply displays live local sensor readings in a small window. It does not capture, record, or transmit your screen, gameplay, or input. All locally stored data remains under your control and can be deleted at any time by removing the folder above through your operating system.

5. Microsoft Store and In-App Purchases

GpuAi is distributed through the Microsoft Store and offers optional premium features as in-app purchases. All purchase transactions and license management are handled by Microsoft. When you download the application or make a purchase, Microsoft may collect data in accordance with its own Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details, billing address, or any other personal information from Microsoft. The application only queries an anonymous license/entitlement state from the system Store APIs to determine which features are unlocked; this check is carried out by Windows, the result is not associated with your identity by us, and no information about you or your device is transmitted to us.

6. Diagnostic Logs (Off by Default, Local Only)

GpuAi writes no log files to disk during normal use; diagnostic output is limited to the attached debugger in development environments. A file-based diagnostic log is created only if you explicitly enable it by setting the GPUI_DIAG_FILE environment variable to a file path, in which case technical events (such as detection steps and error messages) are written to that path on your own device. Such a log is never uploaded or transmitted by the application and can be deleted by you at any time.

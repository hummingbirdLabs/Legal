Privacy Policy

Application: PhotoFilterLab  ·  Effective date: October 4, 2026  ·  Controller: HummingbirdLabs

Short summary: PhotoFilterLab processes all of your photos entirely on your device. The application performs no network communication of its own and has no internet-access capability. Your imported images, edited photos, filter settings, and diagnostic logs never leave your device and are never transmitted to us. We do not have access to your photos or any personal data.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, process, store, or transmit any personal data. PhotoFilterLab is a fully offline desktop application. All photo filtering, color grading, image rendering, and export operations are performed exclusively on your local device. The application does not include any internet-access capability, and no data leaves your device to us.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your imported, edited, or exported photos or image files
  • File names, folder paths, or image metadata (EXIF)
  • Filter parameters, editing history, or application settings (stored locally only)
  • Usage statistics or telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information
  • Device identifiers or hardware information
  • Name, email address, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers

3. How Your Photos Are Handled

When you import a photo into PhotoFilterLab, apply filters or creative looks, and export the result, every step of that processing happens locally on your device using on-device image processing. Your original photos and your edited results are read from and written to locations you choose on your own storage. We never upload, copy, back up, or otherwise access your images.

Local data storage: PhotoFilterLab stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Application settings and preferences (such as theme and export options)
  • A local diagnostic log file (photofilterlab.log)

Imported and exported images remain in the folders you select; the application does not maintain its own copy of your photo library. All locally stored data remains under your control and can be deleted at any time through your operating system.

4. Microsoft Store and In-App Purchases

PhotoFilterLab is distributed through the Microsoft Store and offers optional premium features as in-app purchases. All purchase transactions and license management are handled by Microsoft. When you download the application or make a purchase, Microsoft may collect data in accordance with its own Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details, billing address, or any other personal information from Microsoft. The application only queries an anonymous license/entitlement state from the system Store APIs to determine which features are unlocked; this information is not associated with your identity by us and is never transmitted to us.

5. External Links

The application may contain links to external web pages, such as this Privacy Policy and our Third-Party Notices. Activating such a link opens the page in your default web browser through the Windows shell; the application itself does not perform any network request to open it. Any website you visit may collect data in accordance with its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

6. Crash and Diagnostic Logs (Local Only)

The application writes a diagnostic log file (photofilterlab.log) to its local application folder to assist with troubleshooting. The file contains technical events such as image dimensions, processing steps, and error messages, and it is automatically cleared at every application start so that it only reflects the most recent run. It remains on your device, is never uploaded automatically, and may be deleted by you at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

PhotoFilterLab does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). As we collect no data from any user, this policy applies equally to all users regardless of age.
